Advertising (Google AdMob)

Google Play Advertising Disclosure: This app contains advertisements. The following section provides the disclosures required by Google Play's Advertising Policy and AdMob Publisher Policies.

4.1 Ad Types Displayed

Sky Bubble Burst displays the following ad formats via Google AdMob:

  • Banner Ads – displayed persistently within the game interface.
  • Interstitial Ads – full-screen ads shown at natural transition points (e.g., level completion). A minimum 60-second frequency cap is enforced between auto-triggered interstitials.

4.2 Non-Personalized Ads Configuration

Sky Bubble Burst is configured to request non-personalized ads from AdMob (nonPersonalizedAds: true). This means AdMob does not use your personal data or behavioural profile to select ads shown in this app.

Ad content is filtered to a General (G) audience rating (MaxAdContentRating.g), which ensures only family-appropriate advertisements are served.

4.3 What AdMob May Collect

Even with non-personalized ads, Google AdMob may collect the following types of information automatically as part of serving advertisements:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID / GAID)
  • IP address (used for approximate geolocation and fraud prevention)
  • Device type, model, OS version
  • App identifiers and interaction data (e.g., ad clicks, impressions)
  • Network and carrier information

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Publisher Policies .

4.4 AdMob App ID

Our AdMob App ID is ca-app-pub-8536272432230680~9983882940. This identifier is registered with Google and is used solely to associate ad requests with our application account.

4.5 Opting Out of Interest-Based Advertising

You can limit ad tracking on your Android device:

  1. Open Settings on your Android device.
  2. Go to Privacy → Ads.
  3. Select "Delete advertising ID" or enable "Opt out of Ads Personalisation".

Note: Opting out does not remove ads from the app; it prevents the use of your Advertising ID for ad targeting.

06

Data Retention

All game data (scores, coins, level progress, unlocked items) is stored locally on your device using get_storage. This data persists as long as the application is installed on your device.

Data is automatically removed when you:

  • Uninstall the application
  • Clear the application's data via Android Settings → Apps → Sky Bubble Burst → Clear Data

Because we do not receive or store any data on our servers, we have no server-side data to delete upon request. There is no retention schedule to manage on our end.

Data retained by Google AdMob is subject to Google's data retention practices .

07

Data Security

Since all game data resides only on your device, its security is protected by your device's built-in application sandboxing (Android's app isolation model). Third-party applications cannot access this data without root-level access to your device.

We do not transmit game data over the network. The only network traffic generated by the app relates to AdMob ad requests, which are handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK using industry-standard HTTPS encryption.

We recommend keeping your device updated with the latest security patches and using a screen lock to protect your device from unauthorised access.

Children's Privacy

Families Policy Note: Sky Bubble Burst is described as a "Kids Game." This section reflects our commitment to children's privacy and our AdMob configuration for general audiences.

Sky Bubble Burst is a family-friendly bubble-popping game suitable for all ages, including children. We take children's privacy seriously and have implemented the following protections:

8.1 No Personal Data Collection from Children

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR) — or from any user, regardless of age. No account creation, name, email, or contact information is ever requested.

8.2 Child-Safe Advertising Configuration

Our AdMob integration is configured with:

  • Maximum ad content rating: G (General) – only family-appropriate ads are served (MaxAdContentRating.g).
  • Non-personalized ads – ad serving does not rely on user profiling or interest-based targeting.
  • tagForChildDirectedTreatment is set to unspecified and tagForUnderAgeOfConsent is set to unspecified.
⚠ Google Play Families Policy Action Required: If you intend to target children under 13 as a primary audience on Google Play, you must set tagForChildDirectedTreatment to yes in your AdMob configuration. This ensures COPPA-compliant ad serving. Please consult Google's Families Policy guidance and update this policy accordingly.

8.3 Parental Guidance

Parents and guardians who have questions about their child's use of Sky Bubble Burst, or wish to request deletion of any locally stored data, may contact us at developerpandav16@gmail.com. Data can also be deleted immediately by clearing the app's data through Android Settings.

Data Deletion / Account Deletion

Google Play Requirement: Google Play requires all apps to provide a data deletion mechanism. The instructions below satisfy this requirement.

Sky Bubble Burst does not have user accounts. All data is stored locally on your device.

To delete all app data immediately:

  1. Open Settings on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to Apps (or Application Manager).
  3. Find and tap Sky Bubble Burst.
  4. Tap Storage.
  5. Tap Clear Data → Confirm.

This action permanently deletes all scores, coins, level progress, unlocked styles, and unlocked backgrounds stored by the app. This is equivalent to a full data deletion request.

Alternatively, uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data automatically.

If you have any concerns or require assistance, contact us at developerpandav16@gmail.com.
