=====================================================
  SKY BUBBLE BURST — FEATURE TEST GUIDE
  For testers who want to check every feature
=====================================================


─────────────────────────────────────────────
 1. APP LAUNCH
─────────────────────────────────────────────
• Open the app.
• You should see the splash screen directly — no white screen or logo flash before it.
• A loading bar fills up at the bottom of the splash screen.
• After it completes, the Home screen opens automatically.


─────────────────────────────────────────────
 2. HOME SCREEN
─────────────────────────────────────────────
• Home screen shows a scenic background image.
• At the top you see a trophy pill — it shows your best score (or "No record yet" if new).
• There is a glowing PLAY button in the center.
• Below it are two buttons: PROGRESS and STYLE.
• When you come back to Home from the game, the screen should appear instantly at full
  brightness — no fade-in or low-opacity flash.


─────────────────────────────────────────────
 3. BUBBLE STYLE PICKER
─────────────────────────────────────────────
• On the Home screen, tap the STYLE button.
• A sheet slides up showing all available bubble styles.
• Each style shows a live bubble preview that bobs up and down.
• Tap any style — it gets a checkmark and the sheet closes.
• The STYLE button emoji updates to match what you selected.
• Your choice is saved — even if you close and reopen the app.


─────────────────────────────────────────────
 4. PLAY BUTTON (pip decoration)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
• Look at the PLAY button on the Home screen.
• There is a small white pill (pip) visible at the bottom edge of the button.
• The button pulses gently — it slightly grows and shrinks on its own.


─────────────────────────────────────────────
 5. GAME SCREEN — BASICS
─────────────────────────────────────────────
• Tap PLAY to start a game.
• Bubbles of different colors float up from the bottom.
• Tap any bubble to pop it and earn points.
• Your score and current level are shown at the top left.
• Your remaining hearts (lives) are shown next to the level.
• The background image changes each new game (14 different scenes).


─────────────────────────────────────────────
 6. SOUND TOGGLE (Silent Mode)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
• During a game, look at the top-right corner of the screen.
• There is a sound icon (speaker) next to the pause button.
• Tap it once — the icon turns red and all bubble sounds stop immediately.
• Tap it again — sound comes back and bubbles pop with audio again.
• Default is ON (sound playing) every time you start the game.


─────────────────────────────────────────────
 7. PAUSE MENU
─────────────────────────────────────────────
• During a game, tap the pause button (top-right corner).
• A pause overlay appears showing your current score and hearts.
• Tap "Resume" to continue the game from where you left off.
• Tap "Go Home" to exit to the Home screen.


─────────────────────────────────────────────
 8. LEVEL UP
─────────────────────────────────────────────
• Keep popping bubbles — as your score grows, the level increases.
• A green "Level Up!" banner slides in from the top when you level up.
• Bubbles get faster and harder as the level goes up.


─────────────────────────────────────────────
 9. HEARTS (LIVES) SYSTEM
─────────────────────────────────────────────
• You start with 8 hearts. Missing a bubble costs a heart.
• When all 8 hearts are used up, a "Hearts Over!" popup appears.
• Tap "Reset Hearts" to refill all hearts and keep playing.
• Tap "Go Home" to return to the Home screen (not Close App).


─────────────────────────────────────────────
 10. GAME OVER
─────────────────────────────────────────────
• When the timer or game condition ends, the Game Over screen appears.
• It shows your final Score and Level reached.
• Tap "Play Again" to start a new game immediately.
• Tap "Leaderboard" to view your progress history.
• Tap "Home" to go back to the Home screen.


─────────────────────────────────────────────
 11. PROGRESS SCREEN — DATES TAB
─────────────────────────────────────────────
• On the Home screen, tap PROGRESS.
• The Dates tab opens by default showing all days you played.
• Each card shows the date, total score, games played, and best score for that day.
• Tap any card to expand it and see individual game results.
• Today's card has a "TODAY" badge and a quick Play button.
• Use the calendar icon to filter by a date range.


─────────────────────────────────────────────
 12. PROGRESS SCREEN — WEEK TAB
─────────────────────────────────────────────
• Tap the "Week" tab at the top.
• You see a bar chart or progress rows for each day of the current week.
• Use the left/right arrows to navigate to previous weeks.
• If you are not on the current week, a "Reset to This Week" button appears
  with some space above it (top padding). Tap it to jump back to today's week.


─────────────────────────────────────────────
 13. PROGRESS SCREEN — MONTH TAB
─────────────────────────────────────────────
• Tap the "Month" tab at the top.
• You see all 12 months of the selected year with progress bars.
• Use the arrows to navigate between years.
• If you are not on the current month, a "Reset to This Month" button appears
  with space above it. Tap it to return to today's month.


─────────────────────────────────────────────
 14. BEST SCORE SYNC
─────────────────────────────────────────────
• Play a game and get a score.
• Come back to the Home screen.
• The trophy pill at the top should update instantly to show your new best score
  (if it is higher than before).


─────────────────────────────────────────────
 15. BACKGROUND IMAGES IN GAME
─────────────────────────────────────────────
• Play several games in a row.
• Each game should show a different background scene.
• There are 14 unique backgrounds total — they rotate without repeating
  until all 14 have been shown.
